I got on the bus and my friends were there,
I looked so tired they could only stare.
Finally, I heard one of them say,
“What did you do yesterday?”
So I told them about the day that I had,
and how it went from good to bad.
[7:00] I got out of bed and woke my brother,
I rushed downstairs and hugged my mother.
I made some breakfast and cleaned my room,
then swept the floor with my witch’s broom!
[10:00] I read a story about Halloween,
with the scariest pictures you’ve ever seen.
I drew a dinosaur with a big long tail,
and huge strong teeth, as sharp as nails.
[12:00] I ate some lunch and had a rest,
and then I studied for my test.
I played the piano for an hour and a half,
then watched television shows for a laugh.
[2:00] After that, Mom made me mop the bathroom,
and clean all the floors with the vacuum.
Even that wasn’t enough—she made me clean the bookcases,
and dust off all of her flower vases.
[3:00] I was getting tired, I was ready to drop,
but I had so much to do, I just couldn’t stop.
I cleaned the car and dropped off the mail,
then I went back inside and cut my fingernails.
[5:00] I ate my dinner, then following Mom’s wishes,
I put away the leftovers, and washed the dirty dishes.
I watered the plants, and fed the dog and cat,
then fixed a hole that was in Dad’s favorite old hat.
[7:00] It was break time again, so I had a quick bite,
it was already moving on towards night.
I took a shower and washed my hair,
then went back to my room and rested there.
[9:00] It still wasn’t time to go to bed,
so I picked up my book and read instead.
I fell asleep, and dreamed about the day I had,
I never want another like it—it was BAD!
[結尾] My story is finished, but I’m sure you will agree,
there’s nothing so good as being free!
My busy, busy day tired me out and made me crazy,
but at least no one can ever say I’m lazy!
