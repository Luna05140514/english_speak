[p. 2-3] Joe has a frog named Jumpy, a two-year-old puppet.
Jumpy has green skin and two big eyes,
but he doesn’t have any legs.
Jumpy cannot jump!
That’s all right because Joe always carries Jumpy with him everywhere he goes.
Jumpy makes Joe feel wonderful.
[p. 4-5] Last Sunday evening, Joe, Mom and Dad went to the night market.
Joe was very excited because there were a lot of good things to eat and drink there.
Jumpy was with Joe, as usual.
Then suddenly, the wind blew hard and took Jumpy away from Joe!
[p. 6-7] Jumpy cannot jump, but now he could fly.
Jumpy looked up and saw lots of stars.
He looked down. There were lots of stars there, too!
[p. 8-9] The wind became stronger.
Two girls were eating stinky tofu at a stand.
They loved the smell.
“Look, there’s a flying frog!” one girl shouted.
“How does it look?”
“It looks really scared . . . Oh, it flew away.”
The wind stopped. Jumpy fell onto the road in front of an ice cream stand.
A mean vendor picked up the puppet.
“Yikes! It feels soft,” she yelled as she threw it onto the ground.
[p. 10-11] A little girl found Jumpy and picked him up softly.
“Oh, it’s a present for me!” said the little girl.
“No, it’s not yours!” yelled her dad.
“Put down that green thing and finish your pudding!”
Just then, three boys walked by.
“There’s an ugly frog!”
“How does it smell?”
“It smells gross.”
The short boy kicked Jumpy.
Jumpy flew into the gutter.
[p. 12-13] The gutter was full of all kinds of garbage and horrible smells.
Some rats even swam happily around Jumpy.
Jumpy felt cold and alone.
It was the worst day of his life.
[p. 14-15] Through a hole, Jumpy saw the sky.
He saw some shiny pieces of candy and a big cookie.
[p. 16-17] All of a sudden, something covered the sky.
Jumpy couldn’t see anything.
After a few seconds, he knew it was Joe!
[p. 18-19] Jumpy was saved by Joe!
“Do you still like your puppet?” asked Mom.
“Of course!” Joe answered.
“Even though it looks dirty and smells gross?” added Dad.
“I’ll always love Jumpy.”
[p. 20-21] Mom and Dad helped Joe wash Jumpy.
Now Jumpy the puppet is cleaner than before.
“He looks great and smells wonderful again!”
