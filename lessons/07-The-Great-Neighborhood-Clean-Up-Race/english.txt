[p. 2-3] Once upon a time,
there was a rabbit and a turtle.
Rabbit and Turtle were very different.
[p. 4-5] Turtle moved slowly.
He liked to take his time.
Rabbit moved quickly.
He was always in a hurry.
Turtle spoke quietly.
He didn’t like to bother the other animals.
Rabbit talked loudly and he bothered all of the animals.
[p. 6-7] “You can run fast, Fox,
but I am much faster than you!”
Rabbit said.
Fox just growled angrily at him.
“You can crawl quickly, Snake,
but I am so much faster than you!”
Rabbit said.
Snake just hissed loudly at him.
[p. 8-9] Rabbit told all of the animals about how fast he could hop.
When he came to Turtle, he said,
“You can’t run fast at all, Turtle,
I am so much faster than you!”
Turtle hid in his shell,
but Rabbit wouldn’t stop talking.
[p. 10-11] “Rabbit, how do you know you are faster than me?”
Turtle said grumpily.
“Well, Turtle,
your legs are short and your shell is heavy!
My legs are strong.
I just know that I am faster!”
“Well, Rabbit,
let’s race to the bottom of the big hill,”
said Turtle.
Rabbit laughed,
“A race?
Yes, a race!
I will easily win this race!”
“We’ll see,”
said Turtle.
[p. 12-13] “I’ll give you a head start!”
laughed Rabbit.
“You start walking and I’ll go tell everyone to meet us at the finish line!”
So Turtle started walking.
He walked slowly but steadily.
He also walked happily
because he didn’t have to listen to Rabbit now.
[p. 14-15] It didn’t take Rabbit long to catch up to Turtle.
Rabbit started hopping in circles around Turtle.
Turtle looked straight ahead
and kept walking slowly and steadily.
“See you at the finish line!” shouted Rabbit.
[p. 16-17] Rabbit was really fast.
He hopped quickly over the log across the creek
and made it all the way to the top of the hill.
When Rabbit got to the top,
he saw that the other animals weren’t at the finish line yet.
“Well, Turtle is so slow.
I think I will take a nap under this tree.
I want everyone to see me win!” Rabbit said.
[p. 18-19] Turtle was slow but steady.
He carefully crawled over the log across the creek
and slowly made it all the way to the top of the hill.
When Turtle got to the top of the hill,
he kept on going all the way down to the bottom.
Slow and steady.
[p. 20-21] Turtle crossed the finish line
and everyone cheered loudly for him.
They cheered so loudly that Rabbit woke up.
Turtle was so surprised he won,
but not as surprised as Rabbit!
